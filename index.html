Basic Computer Skills for Seniors

Computers should work for you, not the other way around. Lessons are slow, friendly and focused on the things you actually want to do. Ask as many questions as you like, as many times as you like.

  • Using a mouse, keyboard, touchscreen and your files
  • Email, video calls and staying in touch with family
  • Browsing the internet and searching safely
  • Spotting scams and keeping your information safe
  • Photos, online banking, appointments and shopping
  • Your own phone, tablet or laptop, set up your way

Good to know

  • One-on-one, at your pace
  • Large text and written notes to keep
  • Online or in person

AI Skills: Beginner to Advanced

AI tools can save you time and spark ideas, but only if you know how to use them. I teach practical skills you can use the same day, along with how to stay safe and think critically about what AI tells you.

Beginner

  • What AI is, and what it isn't
  • Chatting with AI assistants like Claude
  • Writing good questions (prompts)
  • Everyday uses: writing, planning, learning
  • Privacy and fact-checking

Advanced

  • Prompting techniques for better results
  • Working with documents, data and images
  • Automating repeat tasks and workflows
  • Building custom assistants and tools
  • Using AI in your work or business

Good to know

  • For adults, teens and teams
  • Hands-on, using your own projects
  • Online or in person
  • Lessons tailored to your goals

Coding for Kids

Kids learn best when they're making something they care about. Lessons are playful and project-based, so children build real games while learning how to think like programmers.

Drag-and-drop blocks to make animations and games. A great first step into coding.

Roblox Studio

Design and script your own worlds and games, then share them with friends.

Minecraft Education

Use code to build, automate and explore inside the game kids already love.

Kids pick up skills like problem-solving, logic and creativity, and have fun along the way.
